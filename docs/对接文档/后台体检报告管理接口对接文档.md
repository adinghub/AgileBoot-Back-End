# 后台体检报告管理接口对接文档

## 1. 接口前缀

后台体检报告管理接口统一前缀：

`/health/reports`

## 2. 报告列表

### 接口

`GET /health/reports`

### 查询参数

1. `pageNum`
2. `pageSize`
3. `ownerUserId`
4. `memberId`
5. `reportType`
6. `parseStatus`
7. `keyword`

### 列表字段

1. `reportId`
2. `memberId`
3. `memberName`
4. `reportName`
5. `reportType`
6. `reportDate`
7. `parseStatus`
8. `aiSummaryStatus`
9. `analysisSummary`
10. `createTime`

## 3. 报告详情

### 接口

`GET /health/reports/{reportId}`

### 详情字段补充

1. `hospitalName`
2. `fileUrl`
3. `originalFileName`
4. `ocrStatus`
5. `ocrTextSnapshot`
6. `ocrTime`
7. `aiSummaryContent`
8. `aiSummaryTime`

## 4. 指标列表

### 接口

`GET /health/reports/{reportId}/items`

### 返回字段

1. `itemId`
2. `itemCode`
3. `itemName`
4. `resultValue`
5. `resultUnit`
6. `referenceText`
7. `abnormalFlag`
8. `abnormalFlagName`

## 5. 分析摘要

### 接口

`GET /health/reports/{reportId}/analysis`

### 返回字段

1. `totalItemCount`
2. `normalItemCount`
3. `lowItemCount`
4. `highItemCount`
5. `abnormalItemCount`
6. `unknownItemCount`
7. `summary`
8. `abnormalItems`

## 6. 异常建议

### 接口

`GET /health/reports/{reportId}/advice`

### 返回字段

1. `memberId`
2. `memberName`
3. `abnormalItemCount`
4. `hasActiveMedicationPlan`
5. `activeMedicationPlanCount`
6. `summary`
7. `adviceItems`

## 7. 趋势对比

### 接口

`GET /health/reports/{reportId}/trends`

### 返回字段

1. `reportId`
2. `memberId`
3. `memberName`
4. `reportType`
5. `comparedReportCount`
6. `items`

## 8. 重新生成 AI 智能总结

### 接口

`POST /health/reports/{reportId}/ai-summary`

### 返回字段

1. `reportId`
2. `aiSummaryStatus`
3. `summary`
4. `disclaimer`
5. `processedTime`

### 说明

1. 若已配置外部大模型，优先返回外部模型增强结果
2. 若外部模型不可用，则自动回退到本地规则总结

## 9. 删除报告

### 接口

`DELETE /health/reports/{reportId}`
